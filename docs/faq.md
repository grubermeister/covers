# Frequently Asked Questions

## What can I do as a guest?
Guests can browse the catalog and documentation pages. Actions that modify data, such as submitting contributions, require authentication.

## Where can I find definitions for system terms?
Open the Glossary article in Help. It explains commonly used terms such as cover, marking, contribution, and citation.

## How do I submit a correction or new record?
Sign in and use the contribution workflow to propose your change. Contributions are reviewed before being published.

## Why is my contribution not visible yet?
New submissions require Editor review before they are published. You can follow each submission's status (draft, pending, approved, needs revision, or rejected) from the Contribute page while signed in.

## What should I include in a high-quality contribution?
Provide clear structured details, include supporting references when possible, and attach evidence that helps Editors validate the proposal.

## Can I edit or delete an existing catalog entry directly?
Regular users cannot directly edit live records. Instead, submit a contribution or update request that goes through Editor moderation.

## How do I report an error in the catalog or the documentation?
For catalog data, sign in and submit a correction through the contribution workflow -- that is the fastest path to a fix, because it goes straight to the Editors. For errors in the documentation itself, contact the project team: beta participants can use the channel they were given when they joined, and anyone else can reach the project through the U.S. Philatelic Classics Society. Include the document title and the section that appears wrong.
